Table takes `rowHref` and `onrowclick`, so a whole row is pressed: the row's name, its `rowHeader` cell or its first, becomes the link or the button, and a press anywhere else on the row presses it, keys and all, but on a link or button of a cell or at the end of a text selection. A whole block of a stacked table is a link, as a list's row is. A pressed row takes the pointer and the hover wash.
