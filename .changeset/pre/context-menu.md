ContextMenu: the actions on an element, opened where it is right-clicked, or below it with Shift+F10 or the ContextMenu key. Spread the attributes of its `target` snippet onto the element, or onto every row of a list or a Table (through `rowAttributes`); `onopen` receives the element it opened on. The menu is a Menu's, with the same items and keys, kept inside the window, and named by `label`.
