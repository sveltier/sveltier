A `List` with `nav` in `AppBar`'s `nav` lays out in a row: the rounded rows and the active wash it has in the drawer, each row as wide as its text, and `ListItem active` marked as the current page (`aria-current="page"`). The same links serve the bar and the drawer, so a `modal` drawer and the bar can share one snippet.
