Select: one value chosen from a list that opens under the field, as the APG select-only combobox. The field keeps focus while the arrows, Home, End and the Page keys move through the options, typing the start of a label goes to it, and Enter, Space, a click or Tab chooses. Items can be grouped (`group`), carry a `description`, be disabled, and be drawn your own way with the `option` and `selection` snippets, which receive the item with any extra properties. It takes the props, defaults and validation of the other fields, and `name` submits the value.
