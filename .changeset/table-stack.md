Table: `stack` shows each row as a block when the table's box is narrower than `'sm'` (600px), `'md'` (960px) or `'lg'` (1280px). A block puts the row header and the columns marked with the new `primary` prop on its first line, and every other column under them as its label and value; the sortable headers become a row of buttons. It goes by the width of the box, in CSS only, and keeps the table's roles.
